About Us

Khabri Junction is a digital news platform focused on Durg, Bhilai, Raipur, Bilaspur, Kawardha, Khairagarh, Rajnandgaon and wider Chhattisgarh updates. We publish local news, public-interest information, politics, crime, health, jobs, sports, weather and market updates for everyday readers.

Our aim is to make local information easy to find, simple to read and useful for people who want quick updates from Chhattisgarh and India.

What We Cover

  • Local civic updates, public notices and city-level developments.
  • Crime, politics, jobs, education, health, sports and entertainment news.
  • District-focused coverage for important Chhattisgarh locations.

Our Editorial Promise

We try to keep headlines clear, avoid misleading claims and update stories when new verified information is available. Readers can report issues through our correction process.
