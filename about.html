About Us

Khabri Junction is a digital news platform focused on Durg, Bhilai, Raipur, Kawardha, Khairagarh, Rajnandgaon, Bilaspur and wider Chhattisgarh updates.

We publish local news, sports updates, public interest reports, market information, jobs, health, politics, crime reports and community stories.

Our Mission

Our goal is to provide fast, simple and useful news updates in English and Hindi for local readers.
