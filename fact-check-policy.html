Fact Check Policy

Khabri Junction reviews sensitive claims before publication, especially when stories involve public safety, crime, health, elections or viral social media content.

Verification Process

We compare available evidence with official records, on-the-record statements, visual context, source history and publication time before presenting a claim as verified.

Developing Claims

If a claim cannot be confirmed, we either hold publication or describe it with careful attribution and clear uncertainty.

Images And Video

We try to confirm the location, date and context of visuals before using them in news stories, thumbnails or web stories.

Reader Feedback

If you spot a factual issue or misleading claim, contact us with supporting evidence so the newsroom can review it quickly.
