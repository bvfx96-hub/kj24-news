Editorial Policy

Khabri Junction focuses on public-interest reporting from Chhattisgarh with priority coverage for Durg, Bhilai, Raipur and nearby districts.

Our Standards

We aim to publish clear, factual and timely reports. Headlines, summaries and visuals should match the actual story and avoid exaggerated claims.

Sourcing

We use a mix of field inputs, public records, official statements, agency material and verified digital sources. When an update is still developing, we label it carefully.

Editorial Independence

Advertising, sponsorship and business relationships do not decide editorial conclusions. Promotional content is separated from news content.

Updates

Stories may be updated when new verified details become available. Important changes are reflected in the article update time.
