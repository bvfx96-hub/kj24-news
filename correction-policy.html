Correction Policy

Khabri Junction corrects factual errors as quickly as possible when reliable evidence shows a published detail is incomplete, unclear or incorrect.

What We Correct

We review errors related to names, dates, places, figures, quotes, context, captions and linked references.

How Corrections Appear

When a correction is made, the story may be updated directly and the revised timestamp will reflect the change. Major corrections may also be noted in the article copy.

How To Report An Error

Email khabrijunction24@gmail.com with the article link, the issue you found and any supporting source.

Review Time

We aim to review reported errors promptly, with priority for breaking news, public-interest updates and legal or safety-sensitive information.
